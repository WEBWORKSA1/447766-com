# 447766.com — Research Brief & Business Case

_Compiled 2026-10-01. Sources listed at the end._

## 1. What "447766" means in Chinese culture

| Digit | Mandarin | Cantonese | Sounds like / association | Sentiment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 四 | sì | sei3 | 死 sǐ "death" (tetraphobia). Lucky in Teochew & Shanghainese (≈ 喜 "happiness") | Negative (mainstream) |
| 7 七 | qī | cat1 | 起 "rise", 齐 "together", 气 "vital energy" — also 欺 "deceive"; 7th lunar month = Ghost Month; 逢七必变 "on seven, things change" | Mixed / transformational |
| 6 六 | liù | luk6 | 溜/流 "smooth, flowing" (Mandarin), 禄 "prosperity" (Cantonese); 六六大顺 "everything goes smoothly" | Positive |

**Pairs**
- **44 — 死死**: the most avoided pair for Cantonese/Mandarin speakers (Teochew exception — e.g. Heng Pang Kiat's No. 44 Emerald Hill, Singapore).
- **77 — 七七**: Qixi, the "Double Seventh" festival (Chinese Valentine's Day; 19 Aug 2026, 8 Aug 2027) — but also 做七/断七, the 49th-day mourning rite.
- **66 — 六六大顺**: strongly auspicious; used in weddings, business openings, prices.

**447766 as a sequence**: no established idiom exists. A native reader hears 死死·七七·顺顺. The usable brand narrative is the arc **4 → 7 → 6: endings → rising/change → smooth success** ("Turn your 4s into 6s"). This must be created and owned deliberately — it is a *comeback/transformation* story, which is emotionally strong and differentiating.

**Other facts about the string**
- `#447766` is a muted teal-green (RGB 68,119,102) → used as the brand colour.
- "+44 7766" = the UK 07766 mobile range (Vodafone UK) — incidental, not a brand angle (avoid phone-lookup/privacy liability).
- No USPTO/WIPO registration found for "447766" in web searches (confirm directly before scaling).
- 447766.com currently resolves to a GoDaddy "make an offer" lander.

## 2. Economics: why numbers are money in China

- **Tetraphobia has a price.** 39 Conduit Road (Hong Kong) skips 43 floor numbers. Sichuan Airlines paid ¥2.33M (~US$280k) for +86 28 8888 8888 (2003). Hong Kong plate "28" sold for HK$18.1M (2016), "W" for HK$26M (2021).
- **Numeric domains are a real asset class.** All 1,000,000 six-digit .com names (6N.com) were registered by Nov 2015; floors moved ~$20 → $170+ in weeks. 2015 data: names with a "bad" 4 traded ~$40, no-4 names ~$80, lucky patterns (88/888) $100–261. Record numeric sales: 360.com $17M (2015), 55.com $2.3M, 114.com $2.1M.
- **Why Chinese brands use numbers:** easier than pinyin, dialect-neutral, phrase-like (51job = "I want a job", 163/126 NetEase, 58.com, 4399.com ≈ 9M visits/mo, 7k7k.com). Numeric brands earn ~50% direct traffic (memorability).
- **Implication for 447766.com:** low resale value as a Chinese "chip" domain, **high value as a memorable brand** for a content/tools property — i.e., build, don't flip.

## 6. Risks & mitigations
- **Thin programmatic content** → index only numbers with unique data (0–99, repeating families, curated combos); each page computes unique breakdown, related set and FAQ.
- **Cultural accuracy** → mark readings as traditional associations; dialect notes; invite native-speaker corrections.
- **"Entertainment only"** disclaimer on all readings; no gambling/lottery predictions.
- **Contests** → official rules, no purchase necessary, skill-testing question, Québec clause.
- **Trademark** → original brand marks only; no third-party logos; see /disclaimer/.
